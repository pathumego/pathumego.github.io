Knowledge projects

Typoday 2017

I led the international Typoday 2017 event, successfully securing funding, partnerships, and global promotion. This initiative created new opportunities and collaborations in typography for Sri Lanka, ultimately leading to the formation of the Akuru Collective.

The Typography Educators Forum 2017

I conceived and led The Typography Educators Forum, which successfully fostered new teaching techniques for undergraduate design courses. Through this initiative, I empowered graphic design educators to develop fundamental tools for improving typographic execution and crafting skills.

Dutch–Ceylon Typographic Heritage Project

I directed a heritage project dedicated to exploring the shared typographic and printing history between Sri Lanka and The Netherlands. This initiative specifically highlighted 18th-century Sinhala and Tamil type-making by the Dutch Press, fostering significant cultural exchange and knowledge.

Galle Font Project

I led the Galle Font Project, focusing on developing a digital typeface based on 18th-century Dutch Press letterforms for cultural preservation. I directed the initial research and proof-of-concept design, preparing for the open-source release of this historical font revival.

AkuruCon 2018

I orchestrated AkuruCon 2018 in collaboration with the Dutch Embassy, celebrating 280 years since the introduction of movable type in Sri Lanka. This event featured impactful workshops and seminars on shared typographic history, engaging diverse participants from design and public sectors.

Akurugraphy (June–November 2026)

I co-curated Akurugraphy with Kosala Senevirathne and Rajitha Manamperi through Mooniak, at Geoffrey Bawa Space, Colombo. Mooniak’s first major public exhibition, it explores Sri Lankan letterforms across Sinhala, Tamil and English scripts, their beauty, their history and where their future lies in digital systems. Drawing on typefaces, archival research, software development and script standardisation, it asks whether digital infrastructure can ever be truly neutral.

Media projects

Kolonbiya

I am the founder and executive producer of Kolonbiya, a popular YouTube Sinhala drama series with a loyal youth cult following. I conceptualised and co-founded the project and led the creative vision. The show is known for its sharp humour, subtle political commentary, and groundbreaking storytelling that mainstreamed discussions on marijuana usage, mass migration, and psychedelics in subtle artistic ways. The channel has over 180K subscribers with a loyal audience base.
